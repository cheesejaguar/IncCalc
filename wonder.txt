Hi,

Firstly I have to say congratulations on your wonderful calculator. There is one bit of
advise that I would like to take issue with though.  In the Wonder advisor page the 
following note is stated:

Note: We typically recommend that the first wonder purchased be the interstate system 
due to the infrastructure-related bonuses it provides.

This is not economically sound. The IS only provides 8% reductions in Infra related 
costs, which looks good, but at a time when nations are buying Wonders a lot of money 
is not spend on Infra. That means that the Wonders that provide an increase in income 
are much better to get as a first Wonder.

A typical nation will probably be making between 5 and 6M per day (before bills) in the 
30 days between buying the 1st and 2nd wonder. So in those 30 days the total income of 
such a nation would be a maximum of 180M. Out of that pot at least 30M will be spend on 
the 2nd wonder, and probably some more on a donation and military taxes and purchases. 
Lets say that in total this will mean that at least 40M of this 180M will be spend on 
other things than Infra (either upkeep or new purchases).  Therefore at the most 78% of 
this money will be spend on Infra related stuff. That means that the 8% reduction in 
Infra costs translates only into a maximum of being 6.2% "better off" (and if Military 
and Tech spending is much higher then this percentage goes further down).

At the very least the SSS has a better percentage (7.1%), and depending on the exact 
circumstances of a nation the $10 wonders might actually also outperform the IS as a 
first wonder choice.

Feel free to do with this what you like, for the time being I'd like to say thanks for 
the calculator one more time! Great piece of work!
