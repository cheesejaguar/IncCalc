The reason our calc. fails for IE is that when you copy/paste your nation data, the images on the page show up as nothing. This means that your resources/bonus resources, and certain other things wont show up in our calc, which gives a big error in all of our calculations. Firefox will put a placeholder in place of each image which is what the calculator parses in order to grab your nation data. You can manually hack this by creating a text file with your govt/religion/trades/bonuses in the right position.

For government and religion, just place the correct response *in brackets* after the specified text when you paste it. I've shown an example here:
Government Type: 
[Monarchy]
  Monarchy - Your people are happy...

National Religion: 
[Christianity]
  Christianity - Your people are ...


For Environment, simply place [ ] between the colon and the value. It should look something like
National Wonders: ..
Environment: [ ] 3.0
Global Radiation: ..


For DEFCON, you need to type out "[ DEFCON # - ]". Replace # with your defcon level --
DEFCON Level:  [DEFCON 5 - ]
Threat Level: ..


For the trades and bonuses, it's a bit more complicated because admin decided to put a whole bunch of descriptive text in the boxes as well. You'll need to do this:

Connected Resources:  [Aluminum – ] [Cattle – ] ...
Bonus Resources:    [Steel – ] [asdf..

Directly copy and paste what you see above and use it as a template. Also, note that you need to have a single space in between each resource/bonus resource, along with the space*hyphen*space within each bracket. So that's [resource*space*-*space*]*space*[resource...

This takes awhile, but once you do it one time you should save that text for future reference. Sorry, but there's no other way for the calculator to parse your information.
