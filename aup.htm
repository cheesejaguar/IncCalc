Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) sets forth the principles that govern the use by customers of the Web-based products and services provided by Inc Services (Inc.). This AUP is designed to help protect our customers, from irresponsible, abusive or illegal activities.

General Violations

This AUP identifies the actions that Inc. considers to be Prohibited Actions (meaning those actions which create denial of access or impediment of service).

Prohibited Actions: General Terms and Conditions of Use

  • Copying/duplicating the Product or creating subsets or derivative databases from the Database.
  • Assigning, selling or passing along the Product.
  • Publishing or otherwise disseminating the Product or creating subsets or derivative databases from the Database.
  • Providing services for a fee using the Product or any subsets or derivatives thereof.
  • Allowing data from the Product to be made available to others or distributing or transferring Search Results in any form to, or for the benefit, of others.
  • Unauthorized distribution of passwords and/or access codes is strictly prohibited.

Prohibited Actions: Network Attacks
  • Any activities which adversely affect the ability of other people or systems to use Inc. services are prohibited. This includes "denial of service" attacks against the hosting servers and network hosts.

Prohibited Actions: Authentication
  • Attempting to circumvent user authentication or security of any account (aka "cracking") is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to, accessing data not intended for the customer, logging into an account the customer is not expressly authorized to access, or probing the security of the service and hosting network.

Prohibited Actions: Impersonation/Forgery
  • Adding, removing, or modifying identifying network header information (aka "spoofing") in an effort to deceive or mislead.
  • Attempting to impersonate any person by using forged headers or other identifying information.
